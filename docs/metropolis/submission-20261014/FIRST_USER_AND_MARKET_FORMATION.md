## Market-formation hypothesis

An audience can produce initial interest without producing recurring two-sided trading. The proposed pain is not inability to mint: it is coordinating transparent terms, discovery, secured graduation, secondary order execution and continuing counterparties. Reasons a durable market may fail to form include weak ongoing use/interest, concentrated holders, thin inventories, adverse selection and no maker incentive. These are hypotheses and market risks, not interview findings.

The bonding phase provides deterministic primary quoting; the successful Kuru graduation creates a venue and seeds initial liquidity under custody policy. Neither implies independent demand or persistent depth. Invite independent traders only after terms and risks are visible. Maker evaluators must review market ID, quote/base units, tick/min/max size, fee/spread policy, inventory and cancel/fill behavior before committing capital. Do not invent a maker rebate or entitlement absent current contract policy.

## Funnel and milestones

Invited qualified operator → reviewed terms → launch → independent primary participation → graduation → first non-team Kuru order/fill → D1/D7 return. Onboarding begins read-only; instrument wallet/transaction outcomes only with consent and without storing secrets.

| 90 days | Two pilot operators repeat a launch; report D1/D7 return with eligible cohort denominator; external security/ops review before production expansion. | Founder. | Mainnet remains blocked until actual qualification and authorization, regardless of calendar target. |

There is no monetary pilot-volume target. Testnet volume can be generated without commercial demand and should not drive launch quality conclusions. Reward mechanisms, referrals, fee changes and paid partnerships require separate qualification; none is silently added to frozen economics.

## Measurement

Creator conversion = successful pilot creators / recruited qualified operators. Graduation = graduated / launched pilot markets, including time window and unresolved failures. Independent-fill success = markets with a verified non-team fill / graduated pilot markets. Maker repeat = evaluators quoting again / makers who began evaluation. Referral conversion = activated attributed participants / qualified referred participants for each segment/channel. Retention = D1/D7 returning traders / activated traders eligible for that day. Exclude team traffic, deduplicate addresses/participants using consented definitions, and disclose any simulation or reimbursement.
