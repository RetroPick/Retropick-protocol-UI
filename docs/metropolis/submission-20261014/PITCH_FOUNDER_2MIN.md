## Spoken script

Launching a token is easy. Creating a market that people can keep trading is harder.

Our first customer hypothesis is a Monad community operator who can bring an audience, but has no trading engineering team. Their traders need clear launch terms, early price discovery, and a reliable destination for secondary trading.

RetroPick carries that journey from bond to book.

A creator launches a token with onchain economic terms. Buyers and sellers discover prices on a bonding curve. At graduation, a Coordinator secures the assets and creates the Kuru market. Initial liquidity shares and excess launch tokens go into protected custody. Traders can then place limit orders, receive fills, and cancel orders.

We build market origination; Kuru provides secondary execution; Monad is the settlement infrastructure.

This works on Monad Testnet. Our recorded functional tests include issuance, curve trading, graduation, and a two-wallet order, fill, and cancellation. Public receipts let judges inspect the actual transactions.

Bonding curves and graduation have prior art. Our engineering focus is the committed venue policy, one secured-assets ledger, retryable atomic graduation, and continuity into Kuru trading.

Locked liquidity does not guarantee depth, stable prices, or returns. This is an unaudited testnet candidate.

We have founder-reported early interest, but verified organic trading and retention remain unmeasured. Next, we propose a pilot with five community operators, twenty independent traders, and two prospective makers. We will measure activation, graduation, first independent fills, spreads, depth, and returning traders.

We want introductions to creator communities, Kuru makers, and security reviewers. Help us prove that launches become usable markets.
