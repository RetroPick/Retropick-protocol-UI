# Acquisition and retention operating plan

Paste-ready canonical GTM is the **Go-to-market and user acquisition** section of BOND_TO_BOOK_SALES_COPY.md. This file supplies operational context without introducing a competing answer.

| Stage | Action | Evidence / success measure |
|---|---|---|
| Recruit | Founder invites five eligible Monad community operators from existing consented channels. | Qualified invited/accepted counts and explicit switching reason; no partnership claim. |
| Observe | Interview each and show the read-only product/receipt journey. | Five consented notes; choose/reject reasons versus Nad/Kuru alternatives. |
| Activate | Aim for three reviewed testnet launches; invite twenty deduplicated independent traders. | Successful creator launches / qualified recruited operators; wallet/signing abandonment recorded. |
| Form markets | Measure graduation and first independent orders/fills; invite two maker evaluators. | Graduated / launched; independently filled / graduated; inventory constraints and custody reconciled. |
| Retain | Follow D1/D7 eligible cohorts and repeat maker/operator sessions. | Returning / eligible activated; repeat makers / participating makers; repeat operator launches. |
| Expand | Evaluate SDK/API/embedded distribution only after pilot learning. | Documented needs and working integrations; production remains gated. |

Creator/community referral conversion and Kuru-user referral conversion are measured separately. No bought engagement, wash trading, fake volume, guaranteed return or invented rebate forms the plan. Incentives/reimbursements, if later approved, must be disclosed and excluded from organic-demand claims.

Retention depends on market quality and counterparties, not simply more launches. Review real two-sided depth/spreads, order visibility, freshness and failure recovery weekly. Founder owns commercial decisions; maintainers validate measurement, safety and reliability. MARKET_QUALITY_AND_RISK.md defines denominator/window rules and proposed testnet targets.

The initial willingness-to-use hypothesis remains unvalidated. If the first five interviews show no reason to switch, reconsider the wedge before scaling. If markets deploy but independent fills/continuing depth fail, report that result instead of maximizing vanity launch counts.
