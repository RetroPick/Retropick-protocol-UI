name: retropick-release-evidence
description: Qualify RetroPick fullstack changes with reproducible commands, chain evidence, independent review and explicit release blockers.
---

Read the owning goal and development/launchpad/testing/RELEASE_GATES.md. For each deliverable record commit/ref, exact command, exit status, artifact and coverage limit.

Separate deterministic fixtures, fork tests, live reads/simulations and broadcast receipts. A quote, screenshot or mocked wallet is not a completed economic journey. Missing credentials/funding/tooling means BLOCKED, not PASS.

Preserve HACKATHON_FREEZE and existing gate status. Testnet success does not authorize deployment/main merge or mainnet. Review immutable-contract incidents separately from web/indexer rollback. Do not store secrets or credential-bearing RPC URLs in evidence.
